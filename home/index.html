Parish Yetu: our parish, online

Parish Yetu means “our parish” in Swahili. It is a Catholic parish app and church management system that brings the parish community together: the priest, the parish office, families, Small Christian Communities (Jumuiya) and every parishioner.

Whether you are looking for Mass times at your church, want to read last Sunday's homily, need to offer a Mass intention, or are trying to get a copy of your baptism certificate, Parish Yetu keeps it in one place, on your phone or computer.

Parish Yetu follows how the Catholic Church is organised: dioceses and archdioceses, deaneries, parishes, Small Christian Communities and families. Parokia yetu, kanisa letu: our parish, our church.

For parish priests and administrators

  • Members & families: one register of your parishioners, families and Jumuiya.
  • Sacramental records: baptism, confirmation and marriage, with certificate requests.
  • Mass schedule & events: publish Mass times and take event registrations.
  • Intentions & pastoral care: receive Mass intentions, prayer and visit requests.
  • Announcements: reach the whole parish with news and reminders.

Frequently asked questions

Questions parishioners and parish offices often ask about Parish Yetu.

What is Parish Yetu?

Parish Yetu (“Our Parish” in Swahili) is an online home for Catholic parishes. Parishioners use it to find their church, read the homilies shared by their priest, see Mass times and events, send prayer intentions, request pastoral care and view their own sacramental records. Parishes use it to manage their church community.

How do I find my Catholic parish on Parish Yetu?

Use the church directory above. Search by the name of your parish, your diocese or your town, then choose your church and select Join this church.

How do I join my church on Parish Yetu?

Find your church, then create your account with your name, email address, phone number and a password. As soon as you register you are a member of your church, and your parish office is told that you have joined.

Can I see Mass times and parish events?

Yes. Once you have joined your church you can see its Mass schedule and upcoming parish events, and register for the events you wish to attend.

Are my prayer requests private?

Yes. You can offer a Mass intention or ask your parish to pray with you, and prayer requests stay private.

Can I get my baptism, confirmation or marriage certificate?

You can view your own baptism, confirmation and marriage records in Parish Yetu and request certificates from your parish.

My parish is not on Parish Yetu. What can I do?

Parish administrators can register the parish on Parish Yetu. If you are a parishioner, you can tell us about your parish.

Is Parish Yetu a church management system for parishes?

Yes. Parish Yetu is a Catholic parish management system. It brings members, families, Small Christian Communities (Jumuiya), ministries, the Mass schedule, events, announcements, intentions and sacramental records together in one place for the parish office.
