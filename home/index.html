Everything a parishioner needs, in one place

Once you have joined your church you can use these services, whenever you need them.

God's Word

Read the sermons and homilies shared by your parish priest, with the Scripture passage for each.

Mass & events

See the Mass schedule and upcoming parish events, and register for the ones you wish to attend.

Prayer intentions

Offer a Mass intention or ask the parish to pray with you. Prayer requests stay private.

Pastoral care

Ask for a home visit, counselling or other pastoral assistance from your priest.

Sacramental records

View your own baptism, confirmation and marriage records and request certificates.

Parish announcements

Stay close to what is happening in your church: news, notices and reminders.

Family and community

See your family, your Small Christian Community and the ministries you belong to.

Your profile

Keep your contact details up to date so your parish can reach you.
